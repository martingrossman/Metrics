Current implementation

Controls first, then both linked visuals

The compact branch keeps the score plot and matrix together on mobile and stacks them for scrolling. The tabs switch the graph mode; they do not replace the page with a single selected panel. This sketch intentionally mirrors that existing behavior.

  • The 10% and 50% shortcuts sit beside the sick-portion slider.
