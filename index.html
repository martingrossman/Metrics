Your flu test is positive. What’s the chance you really have flu? That’s precision—and it also depends on how common flu is among the people tested.
Compare 10% and 50% sick using the same test. Precision changes, even though the portion of sick people correctly testing positive (TPR) and the portion of people without flu getting a false alarm (FPR) stay the same.
